Clearview Community TV — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 25, 2026  •  Clearview TV for iOS & iPadOS

Overview

Clearview Community TV ("Clearview TV," "we," or "our") respects your privacy. The Clearview TV app is a free community media app for watching Wakefield, NH Channel 1301 live broadcasts, on-demand town meetings, upcoming municipal schedules, and local Wakefield weather.

We do not require an account, we do not show ads, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, account credentials, or contacts.
  • We do not collect your GPS or precise location. Weather forecasts use the fixed public coordinates of Wakefield, NH (03872).
  • We do not use advertising SDKs, third-party trackers, or Apple's Advertising Identifier (IDFA).

Anonymous App Analytics

To monitor stream reliability and understand general app usage (such as app launches, tab views, and feed or playback errors), Clearview TV uses TelemetryDeck, a privacy-first analytics service.

  • All analytics signals are anonymous and aggregated and are not linked to your identity or device across other apps.
  • No personally identifiable information is sent to TelemetryDeck.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The app saves the following preferences strictly on your device using Apple's local storage:

  • Bookmarked broadcasts (Saved)
  • On-demand playback positions (Continue Watching)
  • App preferences (default playback speed, Low-Data Mode, auto full-screen on rotate, and followed boards for local meeting alerts)
  • Temporary cached broadcast lists and video thumbnails

This data stays on your device (subject only to your own Apple iCloud device backup settings) and can be cleared at any time inside the app under Settings & About → Storage & History.

Optional Device Permissions

  • Calendar (Write-Only): Requested only when you tap "Add Calendar Reminder" on a Wakefield town meeting. Used solely to create that calendar event. The app never reads or uploads your existing calendar events.
  • Notifications (Local Alerts): Requested only if you enable "Local Meeting Alerts." Reminders are scheduled directly on your device without push notification servers or device tokens.

Public Data Sources & External Links

To display live streams, archived broadcasts, schedules, and weather, the app connects over encrypted HTTPS to:

  • Castus Cloud / Amazon CloudFront — Clearview Community TV Channel 1301 live stream and on-demand broadcasts
  • Town of Wakefield, NH (wakefieldnh.gov) — Public municipal meeting calendar and agenda PDFs
  • Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com) — Public 7-day weather forecast for Wakefield, NH 03872

If you tap "Report an Issue / Send Feedback" in Settings, your email app opens a draft addressed to clearviewtvyt@gmail.com with optional non-personal technical diagnostics (app version, iOS version, and last error), which you can edit or discard before sending.

Children's Privacy & Policy Updates

Clearview TV is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. If we update this Privacy Policy, we will post the revised version on this page with an updated effective date.
